Hello! I’m Konstantinos (Kostas for short).

Thanks for stopping by. I’m a hands‑on hardware engineer, who turns hard problems into reliable systems. I design analogue circuits, high‑speed digital logic, embedded firmware, FPGA architectures, and rugged multilayer PCBs built for real‑world use. My projects span medical sensors, automotive electronics, satellite user terminals, and defence systems; work that rewards careful architecture, clear trade‑offs, and relentless testing.

I write production firmware, build FPGA prototypes, and use Matlab and Python for algorithm development and signal processing. I program in C/C++ and VHDL, and I use open‑hardware platforms (Arduino, Raspberry Pi) to accelerate prototyping when it makes sense.
